Carousel of trees

Pine needles beneath snow to the hissing pressure of what sleeps below
Berries and sticks and actual magic
Midnight cathedrals and caricatures of castles
Sparks of stone silhouette behind walls
Mountains move to the old dogs calls
Forgotten gods a statues darling
A raft for two
A thriving garden
Smoking trees move in groups shifting carousels converge at the point the light stops
