Creator of eidolons

What shelters do I build and what do I gain from showing them off?

The presence of a camera haunts this field, a ghost in a poem of apparitions, A shelter criticized yet cradling.

Leaves of grass have said
"Lo, I or you,
Or woman, man, or state, known or unknown,
We seeming solid wealth, strength, beauty build,
But really build eidolons."

Eidolons fill this cave, singing a song of all divinity. flipping a single sided coin of heads and tails. watching mountains of sun and shadow. I see what I see and say what's already been said.

Here ill stay, in from the rain, fashioning eidolons
